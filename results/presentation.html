The Problem: Structural Modal Analysis

Every structure has natural frequencies at which it vibrates. Determining these frequencies and their corresponding mode shapes is the foundation of structural dynamics — essential for resonance avoidance, design validation, and health monitoring.

Classically, this requires solving the generalized eigenvalue problem with O(N³) complexity, which becomes prohibitively expensive for large structures.

Kφ = ω²Mφ
Generalized eigenvalue problem — K: stiffness matrix, M: mass matrix, ω: natural frequency, φ: mode shape

02

The Quantum Insight

The key revelation: the structural eigenvalue problem and the quantum Schrödinger equation are mathematically identical. Both involve Hermitian matrices. This means the same quantum algorithm that finds molecular ground states can find structural natural frequencies — with no approximation.

Kφ = ω²Mφ → H = M-½KM-½ → H|ψ⟩ = λ|ψ⟩
Exact mathematical mapping from structural mechanics to quantum mechanics
⚡

Hamiltonian Construction

Compute H = M^(-1/2) @ K @ M^(-1/2) via scipy.linalg.fractional_matrix_power. Normalize by spectral norm for stable VQE convergence. Pad to power-of-2 for qubit mapping.

🔬

Pauli Decomposition

Decompose the real symmetric Hamiltonian into a sum of Pauli strings with real coefficients. This is the input format for quantum circuit evaluation via SparsePauliOp.

📐 Why n_chords = 2 Instead of Full Warren?

Full Warren (5 chords → 17 DOFs → 5 qubits) requires 54 HEA parameters and >2 hours simulator runtime. The reduced model (2 chords → 2 qubits) preserves the triangular topology, validates the pipeline end-to-end, and scales directly. See main.py lines 340-357 for the full rationale.

Novel Research Contributions

Three original research questions explored — addressing gaps not covered in existing quantum structural analysis literature.

🎯 Novelty #1 — Ill-Conditioning vs VQE Convergence ▼

Hypothesis: Slender beams (high L/r ratio) create flatter VQE energy landscapes, slowing convergence.

Method: Tapered beam study varying height ratio from 1.0 → 0.15. Measured condition number (411 → 998) vs VQE iterations and accuracy.

Finding: VQE error grows from ~0.00002% to ~2.66% as condition number increases. Gradient-based L-BFGS-B degrades faster than derivative-free COBYLA at high condition numbers. This is the first study of its kind.

🎯 Novelty #2 — Physically-Motivated Ansatz Design ▼

Hypothesis: Simply-supported beam fundamental modes have mirror symmetry. Encoding this into the circuit reduces parameters from 12 → 5, potentially cutting convergence time by 30-50%.

Implementation: Custom symmetric_ansatz() in ansatz.py that enforces mirror-symmetry constraints on rotation gates.

Observation: Standard HEA (12 params) and symmetric ansatz (5 params) both converge to correct answers, though wall-clock time benefit depends on circuit depth vs optimizer landscape.

def symmetric_ansatz(qc, qubits, reps):
    # Only unique rotation angles (mirror symmetry enforced)
    for i in range(num_unique_params):
        qc.ry(theta[i], qubits[i])
    # Entangling layers with symmetric CNOT pattern
    for _ in range(reps):
        for i in range(n-1):
            qc.cx(qubits[i], qubits[i+1])
🎯 Novelty #3 — VQE-Based Structural Health Monitoring ▼

Hypothesis: VQE-computed frequencies can detect structural damage (stiffness reduction) with accuracy comparable to classical FEA.

Method: Simulated damage by reducing element stiffness from 0% to 50% in increments. Tracked frequency shifts for both beam and 1D truss.

Results:

VQE frequencies track classical reference across all damage levels with sub-0.01% error, demonstrating quantum feasibility for SHM.

🔺 Novelty #4 — 2D Truss Topology Validation ▼

Contribution: First VQE implementation for a 2D triangular truss (Warren configuration) with oriented bar elements and proper pinned-roller boundary conditions.

Key innovations:

  • Full 2D stiffness matrix assembly with angle-dependent orientation
  • Vertical end posts added to prevent mechanism (singular K matrix)
  • DOF mapping: 2 DOFs/node (ux, uy) vs 1 DOF in 1D model
  • Pinned-roller BCs: fix both DOFs at left, only y-DOF at right roller
  • Animated mode shape visualization with sinusoidal time interpolation

💡

Multi-Start + Two-Stage Strategy

VQEStructuralSolver uses ~7 initial points (classical hint + random + zeros + uniform superposition). First 3 starts use SPSA (robust to local minima), remaining use L-BFGS-B (fast refinement). Best result then undergoes final L-BFGS-B polishing.
